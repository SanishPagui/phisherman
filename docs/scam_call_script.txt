VOICE DEMO — 40-SECOND SCAM-CALL SCRIPT (read from a second phone near the demo phone)

Do NOT use a real person's identity or a real bank name as an actual threat to a real
number. This is a synthetic script for a prototype. Natural order: greeting + authority
claim → threat → app install → OTP → payment demand. Expect the meter to cross AMBER early
and hit RED before the payment demand.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Hello, I am calling from the bank's fraud department. This is an officer speaking.

We have detected a suspicious transaction on your account and your account will be
blocked within two hours if we cannot verify it.

To fix this, please install the AnyDesk app from the link we send so we can check your
screen.

I also need the OTP you just received to confirm your identity — please read it out.

Finally, to keep your money safe, transfer the balance to the secure verification
account I give you now."
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NORMAL-CALL CONTROL SCRIPT (must stay GREEN):
"Hi, are you free this evening? The delivery is arriving around six, please keep the gate
open. Also mum said dinner is ready, so come home early. See you soon."

CUSTOMER-CARE CONTROL (mentions OTP once; should stay green or at most amber):
"I am calling my own bank's helpline about a failed payment. They said an OTP was sent to
confirm the refund, but I did not share it with anyone."

HOW TO USE IN THE APP:
- Live: tap "Start call guard", grant the mic + notification permission, play the script.
- Reliable fallback (recommended for the stage): type/paste each line into the call-guard
  text box and tap "Add line" — the same VoiceAnalyser scores it. (PS4 §9.4/§9.5)
